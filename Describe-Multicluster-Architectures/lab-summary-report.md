#### 1. Multicluster Challenges Identified

- **Monitoring Gaps**: Lack of unified visibility

#### 2. Solutions Implemented
- **ArgoCD**: GitOps-based application deployment
- **OPA Gatekeeper**: Policy enforcement across clusters
- **Prometheus**: Federated monitoring setup
- **Automation Scripts**: Reduced manual operations
